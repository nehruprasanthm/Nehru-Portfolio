Why security

It started with a scam

In my college days my phone was stolen. Not long after, I paid an Instagram clothes seller through GPay and never received anything. I reported it to the cyber cell. Nobody followed up.

That was the day I decided I wanted to understand this world from the inside.

I want to work in red team because I like discovering things. As a kid I wanted to be an astronaut for the same reason. In security, every system might hide a bug nobody has found yet, and finding one is the best feeling I know.

Then I want to tell people what I found, so they know how attackers really work and stay safe.

B.E. Electrical & Electronics Engineering, Bannari Amman Institute of Technology, 2025. The control-systems background is why ICS/OT security interests me too.
